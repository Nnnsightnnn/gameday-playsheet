The One-Sentence Doctrine

You have the worse roster, so turn a 12-possession game into a 7-possession game: bleed clock every snap, take the checkdown, and make scheme conflict (rubs, options, motion) beat talent — one defender wrong beats five ratings points every time.

The five rules

  1. Milk the play clock under 5 on every offensive snap unless trailing late.
  2. Long drives ARE your defense. Every first down deletes 40+ seconds of their offense.
  3. No 50/50 lob throws. Your receivers lose the dice roll. Checkdowns and in-bounds completions — and this year's WR/DB hand-fighting makes contested balls even worse for you.
  4. Add variance only at decision points: go on 4th-and-short past midfield. M27 shipped a dedicated Tush Push play — until it's patched, it's your 4th-and-1 too, not just theirs.
  5. Take points early when drives stall; in the 4th quarter down 4–8, the conversion attempt usually beats the field goal.
Why the Falcons book carries this plan: consensus top-5 offense at M27 launch. Run-first pistol base, the Gun Bunch money page, the best checkdown route in the game (HB ANGLE) for Bijan, and a defensive book that natively carries the full Nickel 2-4 mug family — the one-package tempo answer other books have to fake.

Offense: Where You Live

Four pictures carry the game. Same look, different outcomes — tendency laundering. The under-center Singleback Wing Slot page is the counter-look so you're never 100% gun/pistol.

Pistol Bunch TE — the base HB ZONE / STRONG POWER on the ground, CROSS DRAG and STICK need zero hot routes, and the JET TOUCH PASS pair lives here. Run-pass balance from one look = no free reads.
Gun Bunch — the money page MESH SPOT, SPACING, Y CURL, SMASH RETURN, DIG RETURN, BENCH PIVOT plus HB BASE and HB SLIP SCREEN. Six-plus calls without leaving the formation. Only 7 books have it.
Gun Trips TE Flex — the quick game STICK has been a top stock play four years running: flat vs man/blitz, stick vs zone. HB ANGLE is Bijan on a linebacker — a mismatch every single snap.
Gun Tight Y Off — motion & run DUO, 0 1 TRAP, HB SCISSORS (clean 4-step read), TE CORNER, and the MTN spinner series. The misdirection engine that keeps boxes honest.

Identity sets nobody labs against

Gun Tight Flex (4 books — PA POST SHOT, the man destroyer, plus DOUBLE SPOT); Gun Y Off Trips Close (3 books — "U Trips": Y-OPTION WHEEL, MTN CORNERS, MTN EMPTY HB SLUGGO); Gun Bunch Wide Nasty (FLOOD SWITCH from nasty splits); Gun Empty Chips Quads (chip-protected empty — the safest empty look in the game); Gun Wing Slot Offset (the MTN JET page: touch pass, HB DUO, PA cross — three outcomes off identical motion).

The Script 15 — Openers as Reconnaissance

Walsh scripted openers so his brain was free to watch the defense's answers. Same here. By play 15 you must know three facts: man/zone %, blitz down and side, where his user lives. Those three facts call the rest of the game.

The 10-Branch Adjustment Tree

  1. Man confirmed → MESH SPOT, CROSS DRAG, HB ANGLE, the jet pair; motion Bijan out wide.
  2. Zone confirmed → SPACING, Y CURL, STICK; high-low the flat defender; checkdowns are wins.
  3. Blitzes from a known side → slide protection to it, throw STICK's flat or the slip screen behind it. One completion usually ends the blitzing.
  4. Users the middle → stop feeding him crossers; attack flats and seams; pump the drag, throw SPEED DIG behind him.
  5. Drops 8 all game → HB BASE, HB DRAW, checkdown every time, milk clock, zero shame.
  6. Shows 2-high pre-snap → confirm at the snap before anything deep. M27's disguised shells are good; SMASH RETURN punishes real Cover 2.
  7. Heavy box / manual pinch → HB ZONE WK, OUTSIDE ZONE, jet touch passes, PA CROSS SHOT over the top. Light box (≤6) → run inside, period.
  8. Inside run stuffed → don't abandon the run, change the track: 0 1 TRAP, MTN JET HB DUO, outside zone from the same look.
  9. He spams one concept → find the ONE route beating you, take away exactly that with ONE adjustment. Never stack five adjustments.
  10. Tilting / nothing works → timeout, back to Pistol Bunch TE: HB ZONE + CROSS DRAG. There is no branch called "improvise."

Defense: One Package, Personnel First

Home: Nickel 2-4 Dbl Mug. Both ILBs mugged in the A-gaps every snap — the identical pre-snap picture whether you drop eight or send six. The opponent burns his five seconds worrying about your look instead of executing his. The full zone suite (C3 SKY / SEAM, TAMPA 2, C2 INVERT, C1 HOLE), five 3-deep pressures, and two sim pressures all live here; the match coverages (C3 MATCH, C2 MATCH, PALMS, BUZZ MATCH) are one audible away in base Nickel 2-4 — same eleven men on the field.

The Hurry-Up Protocol

Pre-game (set once, persists through tempo)

  • Auto Flip Defensive Play Call: ON — alignment matches strength when you can't.
  • Ball in Air: Balanced (Play Ball protecting a late lead; never Aggressive).
  • Option Defense: Conservative vs scramblers; RPO Pass Key Conservative vs RPO teams.
  • First defensive series with time: set flat zones to 30 vs crosser-spam, hard flats (10) vs bubble/quick-out teams. Zone drops persist between plays — they keep working during no-huddle.

The 4 pre-set audibles (all Dbl Mug — same personnel, blind-safe)

1 · COVER 3 SKYRun-and-pass sound default.
2 · TAMPA 2Crosser / drag eraser.
3 · COVER 2 INVERTFlat / quick-game eraser, disguised 2-high.
4 · BLITZ LOOP 3The tempo blitz with the 3-deep parachute.

In-play 5-second routine

  1. (1s) Backfield only: empty → SKY or INVERT; RB present → any of the four.
  2. (1s) Hit the audible. Auto-flip handles alignment — don't touch it.
  3. (1s) One global adjustment max. NEVER hot-route an individual defender under tempo.
  4. (2s) Take the mid hook / MLB, breathe, play the middle. Your user IS the disguise.

Panic buttons (never awful vs anything): TAMPA 2 · COVER 3 SKY · and with two extra seconds, COVER 4 PALMS from base 2-4.

Between Plays: The 25-Second Ritual

0–5s
Log, don't feel. One word: "man," "blitz-left," "user-mid."
5–10s
On defense: personnel names the block — count backs + TEs as they break; LIVE only knows down/distance. On offense the LIVE highlight already did it. Read 2–4 options, not forty.
10–15s
Pick and commit. A decent call fully executed beats a perfect call chosen at 0:03.
15–25s
Sheet down, eyes up. Shell, box, user, leverage. Milk to ~3. Snap.

Stillness sharpens sight. The sheet exists so the decision is already made —
and the 25 seconds go to watching, not choosing.
