The One-Sentence Doctrine

This roster punches first: impose wide zone until the defense overcommits, then manufacture explosives off the exact same picture — boots, drag-wheels, deep overs, seams. You're not surviving possessions, you're stacking body blows until the knockout window opens. This is how the real 2026 Falcons are built to play.

The five rules

  1. Run to set up the shot, not to kill clock. 50%+ run rate on early downs is the lever that makes play-action lethal — the 2025 Falcons ran the league's fewest play-action passes and everyone got fired. Don't repeat it.
  2. Every explosive comes off run action. The boot series, PA DEEP CROSS GO, PA DEEP OVER, the drag-wheel — all dressed as HB STRETCH. Earn the bite, then cash it.
  3. Throw driven balls, not moonballs. Penix is 92 THP / 76 DAC: bullet seams, deep digs, benders. The lone lob exception is London 1-on-1 — 97 catch / 97 jump wins the ball, not the placement. (Tua mode: live in the sub-2.5s rhythm game instead.)
  4. Feed the matchup menu. They can't bracket London, carry Pitts's 90 speed up the seam, AND hat Bijan underneath. Read the shell, take what's conceded.
  5. Measured aggression, Stefanski-style: go on 4th-and-2 or less inside their 45 — usually duo or sneak. Take the knockout when it's there; this offense converts.

Offense: The Stefanski Identity

Home base: Singleback Wing Tight. The entire 2026 Falcons offense lives in one formation: HB STRETCH / HB ZONE WK / HB DUO / HB POWER O on the ground, then FAKE HB ZONE QB BOOT, PA BOOT FLOOD, PA BOOT SLIDE, PA WHEEL SLIDE, PA Y-DRAG WHEEL, Y STICK, STICK NOD VERTICAL off the identical picture. (Fittingly, this formation also lives in the Browns and Vikings books — Stefanski's old teams.) Nobody can key run/pass because there is no tell.

The 10-Branch Adjustment Tree

  1. Man confirmed → MESH, drag-wheel, PA POST SHOT; London iso every 3rd down; motion Branch to ID leverage.
  2. Zone confirmed → Y STICK, SPACING, CHOICE PIVOT RETURN, CURL FLAT SEAM; flood the boot side.
  3. Blitzes from a known side → slide to it, PA BOOT SLIDE away from it, slip screen into it.
  4. Users the middle → stop the crossers; Pitts seam PAST him, digs behind him (SPEED DIG lives in Gun Bunch).
  5. Drops 8 all game → keep running STRETCH/DUO — that's a win for the identity, not a stalemate. Bijan eats.
  6. Overcommits to the stretch → the whole point: FAKE HB ZONE QB BOOT, PA Y-DRAG WHEEL, PA DEEP OVER, cutback HB ZONE WK.
  7. Heavy box vs Wing Tight → don't abandon the picture: Y STICK / STICK NOD VERTICAL from the same set, or motion to Y Trips Close jets.
  8. Stretch stuffed by alignment → change track not identity: HB DUO, HB ISO downhill, JET 6 SOAR opposite.
  9. He spams one concept on D → take away exactly that with ONE adjustment; never stack five.
  10. Tilting / nothing works → timeout, back to HB STRETCH + PA BOOT FLOOD. The identity is the reset button.

Defense: The Ulbrich Blueprint

Boring on purpose early, violent on third down. The real Ulbrich defense plays a deliberately binary early-down menu — Cover 3 or quarters on ~75% of snaps, almost never Cover 2 — stops the run with extra hats, forces long yardage, and THEN unleashes the sim-pressure package that produced 57 sacks. That maps perfectly onto Kenny-ball: one tempo-proof home, aggression on schedule.

Your two elite pieces drive everything: Jessie Bates (94, Shutdown X-Factor, 95 play-rec) IS your user — deep post in C3, low-hole robber in the mug looks. A.J. Terrell (88, 95 press) shadows their X. The front four won't win alone (weak DT interior) — pressure comes from scheme: mugs, sims, and Pearce/Walker's 90+ acceleration off wide alignments.

The Hurry-Up Protocol

Pre-game (set once, persists through tempo)

  • Auto Flip Defensive Play Call: ON.
  • Ball in Air: Balanced (Play Ball protecting a late lead; never Aggressive).
  • Option Defense / RPO keys: Conservative as scouted. QB Contain vs scramblers.
  • First free series: flat zones to 30 vs crosser-spam, hard flats (10) vs bubble/quick-out teams. Zone drops persist through no-huddle.
  • CB matchups: Terrell shadows their No. 1 by overall.

The 4 pre-set audibles (all Dbl Mug — same personnel, blind-safe)

1 · COVER 3 SKYRun-and-pass sound default; Bates post.
2 · COVER 3 SEAMSeam-carry C3 vs verts-from-tempo.
3 · TAMPA 2Crosser / drag eraser; Bates poles it.
4 · BLITZ LOOP 3The tempo blitz with the 3-deep parachute.

In-play 5-second routine

  1. (1s) Backfield glance: empty → SKY or SEAM; RB → any of the four.
  2. (1s) Hit the audible. Auto-flip handles alignment.
  3. (1s) One global adjustment max. Never individual hot routes under tempo.
  4. (2s) Take Bates, breathe, play the middle. Your user IS the disguise.

Panic buttons: TAMPA 2 · COVER 3 SKY · (+2s) 3-4 Odd COVER 4 QUARTERS.

Between Plays: The 25-Second Ritual

0–5s
Log, don't feel. One word: "man," "blitz-left," "user-mid."
5–10s
On defense: personnel names the block — count backs + TEs as they break. On offense the LIVE highlight already did it. Read 2–4 options, not forty.
10–15s
Pick and commit. A decent call fully executed beats a perfect call chosen at 0:03.
15–25s
Sheet down, eyes up. Shell, box, user, leverage. Snap when ready — this build plays on schedule, not on the play clock's last breath.

Stillness sharpens sight. The identity is the decision already made —
the 25 seconds go to watching, not choosing.
