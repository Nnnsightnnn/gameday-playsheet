# Iowa Game Plan — CFB 27 Online H2H

*Built Aug 2026 from current meta research (post-1.006 patch). Load it in-app:
Tweaks panel → Game plan → Load. Every play on the sheet carries its coaching
note. This doc is the layer the sheet can't hold: doctrine, protocol, counters.*

---

## The one-sentence doctrine

You have the worse roster, so turn a 12-possession game into a 7-possession
game: bleed clock every snap, take the checkdown, and make scheme conflict
(motion, cheat splits, fake reverses) beat talent — one defender wrong beats
five ratings points every time. Iowa is the rare book that WANTS this plan:
the run-first meta and this playbook point the same direction.

**The five rules**

1. Milk the play clock under 5 on every offensive snap unless trailing late.
2. Long drives ARE your defense. Every first down deletes 40+ seconds of their
   offense.
3. No 50/50 lob throws. Your receivers lose the dice roll. Checkdowns and
   in-bounds completions.
4. Add variance only at decision points: go on 4th-and-short past midfield.
   The sneak meter makes 4th-and-1 a skill play (check for a pinched line
   first).
5. Take points early when drives stall; in the 4th quarter down 4-8, the
   conversion attempt usually beats the field goal.

## Offense: where you live

Four formations carry the whole game — three of them under center, which is
itself the disguise: nobody at your rank defends Singleback variety anymore.
Everything looks the same at the snap; what happens after differs. That is
tendency laundering.

| Formation | Role |
|---|---|
| **Singleback Deuce Close** | Base. 12-personnel everything: stretch, duo, trap, toss, jet motion, real dropbacks (Flood Trail, Spacing Switch) and three PA shots from one picture. |
| **Singleback Wing Close** | The wide-zone hub. Wide Zone + Duo + Zone Toss is the complementary-runs trio, and the whole fake-reverse family (toss fakes, PA Fk Toss Flood/Wheel) launders off the same action. |
| **Singleback Bunch X Nasty** | **Identity.** The cheat-split bunch lives in only ~11 of 130+ books — and the meta labs the Gun version, not this Singleback one. Cheat runs hit gaps nobody practices; the compressed bunch is a rub machine vs man. |
| **Gun Y Trips Wk** | Third-down / 2-minute hub. Mesh, Stick, Strong Flood, Y Sail, Verts Slot Nod, Spacing Snag — plus Buck Sweep and RPO Peek Slant so gun never means pass. |

**Annexes (a few calls each, never full-time):** Gun Trey Open HB Str (alt gun
hub — Mtn Stick Wheel, H Mtn Mesh family, Slot Fade); I Form Wing Over (6-book
jumbo set, the 4-minute-drill hammer); Strong I Close (2-book set — Quick Toss
and the end-around bluffs); Gun Tight Slot Open (HB Direct Snap — one of the
best plays in the series, with a built-in reverse twin); Goal Line.

**Motion on every snap you can afford it.** Iowa carries built-in MTN/MOTION
plays in Deuce Close: defender chases across = man, bump/pass = zone. Motion
on plays where it does nothing too, so it carries no signal.

**Coverage beaters** (when you've ID'd the shell):

- Cover 2: PA Cheat Deep Corner, Strong Flood, Verts Slot Nod — corners over
  the squat CB, seams split the safeties
- Cover 3: Strong Flood, Flood Trail, Mtn Stick Wheel, PA Fk Toss Wheel — the
  wheel behind a streak is still the standing zone cheat
- Cover 1 man: Mesh, H Mtn Mesh Post, Crack Toss, PA Ctr Waggle — rubs win,
  and man can't set the edge vs the crack
- Cover 4: Wide Zone / Mtn Duo until they check out of it; Stick and Quick
  Unders are free underneath
- Blitz/Cover 0: Spacing Snag, Stick hot, HB Slip Screen, HB Mid Draw, Crack
  Toss away from the pressure

**The 2-3 setups-then-counter rhythm:** run the base play until he adjusts,
THEN call the counter. If he never stops the base, never leave it. Hammered
Wide Zone? Now PA Stretch Shot or PA Ctr Waggle. Fed him the toss? Now PA Fk
Toss Wheel. Zone weak from the bunch twice? Now Cheat Counter Y back the other
way.

## The Script 15 (openers as reconnaissance)

Walsh scripted openers so his brain was free to watch the defense's answers.
Same here. By play 15 you must know three facts: **man/zone %, blitz down and
side, where his user lives.** Those three facts call the rest of the game.

- Plays 1-3 — man or zone, find the user: Wide Zone (watch box count), Mtn Duo
  (motion check), Cheat HB Zone Wk (does he align to the bunch?).
- Plays 4-6 — blitz tendency: get to 3rd-and-medium, call Stick or Spacing
  Snag with a hot read. Most opponents trust ONE 3rd-down blitz. Log it.
- Plays 7-9 — user location: one crosser (Mesh), one flat (Flood Trail), one
  seam. Whichever gets user-jumped is his home; the other two are open all game.
- Plays 10-12 — edge discipline: Motion Zone Toss, Hb Toss Fk Reverse, Crack
  Toss. If the fake reverse freezes him once, the PA twins are live all game.
- Plays 13-15 — deep honesty: one max-protect shot (PA Shot). Not to complete
  it — to learn if his safeties are user-controlled and if he drops 8.

## The 10-branch adjustment tree

1. Man confirmed → Mesh, H Mtn Mesh Post, wheels; motion the fast guy; Crack
   Toss at the edge man can't set.
2. Zone confirmed → high-low the flat defender (Stick, Flood Trail); patience;
   checkdowns are wins.
3. Blitzes from a known side → slide protection to it, back releases away,
   throw the slant behind it. One completion usually ends the blitzing.
4. Users the middle → stop throwing crossers at him; attack flats/seams; pump
   the mesh, throw the post behind him (H Mtn Mesh Post is built for this).
5. Drops 8 all game → checkdown and HB Mid Draw every time, milk clock, zero
   shame.
6. Shows 2-high pre-snap → confirm at the snap (safety first-steps) before
   anything deep. This cycle's disguises are good.
7. Heavy box / manual pinch → Stretch, Motion Zone Toss, PA Stretch Shot.
   Light box (≤6) → Wide Zone and Duo, period.
8. Inside run stuffed → don't abandon the run, change the track: Cheat Counter
   Y, HB Mid Draw, stretch from the same look.
9. He spams one concept → find the ONE route beating you, take away exactly
   that with ONE adjustment. Never stack five adjustments.
10. Tilting / nothing works → timeout, back to Wide Zone + PA Ctr Waggle.
    There is no branch called "improvise."

## Defense: one package, four buttons

**Playbook: 4-2-5 Man Pressure** (S-tier, the only book with all four meta
formations). **Home: Nickel 3-3 Cub** — the most agreed-upon formation in CFB
27, in only three books. Pressure comes from alignment stress (mug looks), not
player talent — that's why it works with a worse roster, and the coverage
calls are zone/match so your corners never live on man islands.

Since patch 1.006 your package **persists vs no-huddle**. Tempo can no longer
wipe your personnel. The whole anti-cheese plan is legal now.

**Before every game (menus, zero in-game seconds):**

- Coach adjustments: Auto Flip ON, Base Align ON (same alignment every snap =
  disguise by sameness), Plaster ON, Smart Zones aggressive underneath, Gap
  Integrity Conservative (the run-first meta punishes aggressive fits), RPO
  keys set once you've seen their lean.
- Set the four audibles from Nickel 3-3 Cub to the Base / Tempo block calls:
  **Cover 4 Show 2, Cover 3 Buzz, Tampa 2, Mike Blitz 0.** The first three are
  blind-safe; Mike Blitz 0 is the tempo-breaker you spring once a drive at
  most — never blind, never vs an unscouted formation.
- Build custom-adjustment macros (tempo shell, run fit, 3rd-and-long). Known
  bug: macros sometimes drop one adjustment when fired — confirm the one that
  matters for the down.

**The 5-second tempo routine** (drop steps if short on clock):

1. Audible, don't browse. One of the four. Never open the full menu vs tempo.
2. Glance at your safeties (live bug: some shells misalign — what you see IS
   the call now; play it, don't re-call).
3. One adjustment max: shade underneath vs quick game, over top vs shots.
4. Pre-select your user defender (Tampa 2 hole LB, the robber, the buzz
   safety) before the snap.
5. **Never flip the defense late vs tempo.** The flip bug freezes your user
   ~5s and the slot corner may not travel. Flip the instant they line up or
   not at all — then user a defender on the untouched side.

**Panic buttons, in order:** Cover 4 Show 2 (never awful vs anything), Cover 3
Buzz (worst case is a 4-yard flat), Tampa 2 (mesh/drag scripts die). Blitzing
blind vs tempo is how underdogs die — only 3 Sam Will Blitz is acceptable
blind pressure (3-deep net behind it).

**The heavy tripwire:** two tight ends on the field means OUT of Cub and into
4-3 Even 6-1 (the sheet's vs-Heavy block) immediately. Stretch and toss into a
light mug look is the current ranked killer, and it's how run-first opponents
will attack this package.

**Timeouts are tempo-breakers.** The moment tilt rises — he converts and
sprints to the line — burn one. It breaks his rhythm, opens your full
playbook, and resets you. Keep two in the second half for clock; the rest
exist to be spent on composure.

## The cheese list and counters (this cycle)

| Cheese | Counter |
|---|---|
| Gun Bunch audible spam | Tampa 2 or Cover 4 Show 2 with match checks vs bunch; never press bunch with bad corners; user the first in-breaker |
| Mesh Slot Deep / crosser spam | Cover 3 Buzz (buzz sits in the drag window) or Tampa 2 Drop; user one drag yourself; never chase a drag with a zone defender |
| Four verts / HB seam | Cover 4 Quarters / Palms; user the HB seam pipe — that's the real read |
| Shift → unlimited hot routes exploit | Assume nothing is locked after a shift; play leverage-and-help, not play-art memory; Show 2 match is the least exploitable shell |
| Trips-to-bunch shifts hunting the flip bug | Flip early or not at all; after any flip, user the untouched side; treat the slot corner's side as soft and protect it with your user |
| Stretch / toss spam (run-first meta) | Check to 4-3 Even 6-1: Cover 4 Quarters, Sam Blitz 3; never sit in mug looks vs outside runs; Gap Integrity Conservative kills the cutback |
| Pancake-block runs (broken mechanic, fix pending) | No scheme answer; pursuit angles, don't user-dive into blocks, rally with numbers |
| Scrambling QB / speed boost | Cover 1 Contain (in 3-3 Cub); Plaster ON; contain ends, never all-out blitz a runner |
| Tempo itself | Post-patch it can't wipe your package. The audible-only routine is the counter. It's a composure weapon; the checklist is the armor. |

## Between plays: the 25-second ritual

1. 0-5s — log, don't feel. One word: "man," "blitz-left," "user-mid."
2. 5-10s — situation names the sheet block. The LIVE highlight already did
   this for you. Read 2-4 options, not forty.
3. 10-15s — pick and commit. A decent call fully executed beats a perfect
   call chosen at 0:03.
4. 15-25s — sheet down, eyes up: shell, box, user, leverage. Milk to ~3. Snap.

Stillness sharpens sight. The sheet exists so the decision is already made and
the 25 seconds go to watching, not choosing.
