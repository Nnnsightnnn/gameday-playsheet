## Defense, Nickel 2-4
- COVER 3 SKY: corners deep outside thirds; offense-LEFT safety deep middle; offense-RIGHT safety down to the right curl-flat; SLOT defender to the LEFT curl-flat; both LBs hooks; four rush.
- COVER 3 CLOUD: right corner cloud flat; right safety deep right third; left safety deep middle; left corner deep third; slot left curl-flat; LBs hooks.
- COVER 4 QUARTERS = COVER 4 PALMS (identical diagrams, match): four deep; slot left curl-flat; one LB right curl-flat; other LB middle hook.
- Pre-snap alignment identical across SKY, CLOUD, QUARTERS, PALMS.
- COVER 1 ROBBER: four straight rushers; left safety deep middle; right safety robber ~10; corners, slot, both LBs man.
- COVER 1 CONTAIN: built-in QB spy on the offense-left LB; contain rush on both edges; one deep; rest man.
- TAMPA 2: safeties deep halves; a LB to the deep middle hook; slot and other LB hook/curl; both corners flats; four rush.
- COVER 2 MAN: two deep halves; five under man; four rush.
## Defense, Nickel 2-4 Wide
- COVER 2 MAN: safeties start ~12, deep halves; five under man; four rush.
- COVER 3 MATCH: corners deep thirds; left safety deep middle; right safety right curl-flat; slot left curl-flat; LBs hooks.
- DBL SAFETY BLITZ: both safeties aligned walked down (~6-7) and blitz; six rush; rest man (Cover 0).
- SILVER SHOOT PINCH: slot and one LB blitz, line pinches, six rush; both safeties deep, no zone marks.
- COVER 1 ROBBER PRESS: left safety deep middle; right safety robber; four rush; rest man.
## Offense
- Gun Y Off Trips Close TE WHIP: trips right, TE close. TE (primary): one step up, then flat outward toward the right sideline at ~2 yds. Inside trips WR: quick out ~5 right. Outside trips WR: vertical ~12 then deep post inside. Left single WR: deep post/over. HB: swing to the LEFT flat.
- Gun Bunch Offset SPEED DIG: bunch right. #1 outside: stem to ~15, deep dig back inside ~16. #2: vertical ~8 then deep crosser left at ~15-16. #3 inside: short out right flat. HB: left flat. Left WR: deep corner/post-corner.
- Gun Spread Y Slot Wk DOUBLE SLANTS: 2x2, HB offset left. Left outside (primary): slant/skinny post ~6-8. Left slot: stem ~5 then angles to the middle ~10. HB: left flat. Right slot: out/flat right ~3. Right outside: stem ~5 then slant inside.
## Defense reference (opponent calls)

Both safeties deep halves; both corners cloud flats (~5); both inside LBs hook/curl shaded toward the numbers; both slot/nickel DBs blitz off the edges; three DL rush. 5 rush, 4 under, 2 deep; middle seam between the halves is the void.
