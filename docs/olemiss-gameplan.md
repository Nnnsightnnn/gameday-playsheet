# Ole Miss Game Plan — CFB 27 Online H2H

*Built Aug 2026 from current meta research (post-1.006 patch). Load it in-app:
Tweaks panel → Game plan → Load. Every play on the sheet carries its coaching
note. This doc is the layer the sheet can't hold: doctrine, protocol, counters.*

---

## The one-sentence doctrine

You have the worse roster, so turn a 12-possession game into a 7-possession
game: bleed clock every snap, take the checkdown, and make scheme conflict
(RPOs, rubs, options) beat talent — one defender wrong beats five ratings
points every time.

**The five rules**

1. Milk the play clock under 5 on every offensive snap unless trailing late.
2. Long drives ARE your defense. Every first down deletes 40+ seconds of their
   offense.
3. No 50/50 lob throws. Your receivers lose the dice roll. Checkdowns and
   in-bounds completions.
4. Add variance only at decision points: go on 4th-and-short past midfield.
   The sneak meter makes 4th-and-1 a skill play (check for a pinched line
   first).
5. Take points early when drives stall; in the 4th quarter down 4-8, the
   conversion attempt usually beats the field goal.

## Offense: where you live

Four formations carry the whole game. Everything looks the same at the snap;
what happens after differs. That is tendency laundering.

| Formation | Role |
|---|---|
| **Gun Wide Y Off Trips Stack** | Identity. Ole Miss exclusive — nobody labs it. Full run/RPO suite + dropbacks + HB Direct Snap from one picture. |
| **Gun Tight Open** | Man/blitz beater hub. Compressed spacing = free rubs. Mesh Spot lives here. |
| **Gun Wide Trips / Wide Trips HB Wk** | The conflict machine. Same picture, run strength flips. RPOs, Switch Verts, floods, Levels. |
| **Gun Empty Base Flex** | Tempo + coverage ID. Empty forces them to declare. Hoss Y Juke is the fastest ball-out in the book. |

**Motion on every snap you can afford it.** Ole Miss carries built-in MTN
plays: defender chases across = man, bump/pass = zone. Motion on plays where
it does nothing too, so it carries no signal.

**Coverage beaters** (when you've ID'd the shell):

- Cover 2: Deep Flood, PA Seams, Post Corner Drive — corners + seams split it
- Cover 3: Deep Flood, 619 Sail, Levels — flood the curl-flat, live intermediate
- Cover 1 man: Mesh Spot, Whips Curls, Slot Wheel — drags/whips/rubs win
- Cover 4: run the ball until they die; All Hitch free vs bail corners
- Blitz/Cover 0: Stick, Hoss Y Juke, Triple Slant, every RPO — one-read hot throws

**The 2-3 setups-then-counter rhythm:** run the base play until he adjusts,
THEN call the counter. If he never stops the base, never leave it. Fed him RPO
screens? Now Fake Screen Verticals. Pounded Power O twice at the goal line?
Now PA Waggle.

## The Script 15 (openers as reconnaissance)

Walsh scripted openers so his brain was free to watch the defense's answers.
Same here. By play 15 you must know three facts: **man/zone %, blitz down and
side, where his user lives.** Those three facts call the rest of the game.

- Plays 1-3 — man or zone, find the user: base run (watch box count and which
  defender moves weird), quick game with motion check, PA off the same run.
- Plays 4-6 — blitz tendency: get to 3rd-and-medium, call quick game with a
  hot read. Most opponents trust ONE 3rd-down blitz. Log it.
- Plays 7-9 — user location: one crosser, one flat, one seam. Whichever gets
  user-jumped is his home; the other two are open all game.
- Plays 10-12 — edge discipline: outside run, RPO (watch the conflict
  defender), option.
- Plays 13-15 — deep honesty: one max-protect shot. Not to complete it — to
  learn if his safeties are user-controlled and if he drops 8 on pass downs.

## The 10-branch adjustment tree

1. Man confirmed → crossers, drags, wheels; motion the fast guy.
2. Zone confirmed → high-low the flat defender; patience; checkdowns are wins.
3. Blitzes from a known side → slide protection to it, back releases away,
   throw the glance behind it. One completion usually ends the blitzing.
4. Users the middle → stop throwing crossers at him; attack flats/seams; pump
   the crosser, throw the dig behind him.
5. Drops 8 all game → checkdown and draw every time, milk clock, zero shame.
6. Shows 2-high pre-snap → confirm at the snap (safety first-steps) before
   anything deep. This cycle's disguises are good.
7. Heavy box / manual pinch → stretch, toss, PA shots. Light box (≤6) → run
   inside, period.
8. Inside run stuffed → don't abandon the run, change the track: counter,
   draw, outside zone from the same look.
9. He spams one concept → find the ONE route beating you, take away exactly
   that with ONE adjustment. Never stack five adjustments.
10. Tilting / nothing works → timeout, back to base run + PA pair. There is no
    branch called "improvise."

## Defense: one package, four buttons

**Playbook: 4-2-5** — Ole Miss's stock defensive book, so there is nothing to
switch pregame: pick Ole Miss and every call on this sheet is in your menus.
**Home: Nickel 3-3 Mint** (3-down nickel, same geometry the old Cub home
had). Pressure comes from alignment stress (mug looks), not player talent —
that's why it works with a worse roster, and the coverage calls are
zone/match so your corners never live on man islands.

*Honest caveat: plain 4-2-5 is A-tier, not S-tier — it gives up the 3-3 Cub
and the 4-3 Even 6-1 heavy front. In exchange the whole sheet works with
zero pregame friction. Mint covers ~90% of what Cub did; run downs now lean
on 4-2-5 Over G and 3-4 Tite instead of the 4-3.*

Since patch 1.006 your package **persists vs no-huddle** (re-confirmed fixed
in TU 2.5, July 23). Tempo can no longer wipe your personnel. The whole
anti-cheese plan is legal now — and TU 2.5's timing-catch nerf shrank the
green window into coverage, so playing real shells got a free buff.

*Rebuilt Aug 4, 2026: defense re-curated for the stock 4-2-5 book after
confirming the sheet's old book (4-2-5 Man Pressure) isn't what Ole Miss
carries in regs. Every call validated against the current catalog.*

**Before every game (menus, zero in-game seconds):**

- Coach adjustments: Auto Flip ON, Base Align ON (same alignment every snap =
  disguise by sameness), Plaster ON, Smart Zones aggressive underneath, Gap
  Integrity Conservative, RPO keys set once you've seen their lean.
- Set the four audibles from Nickel 3-3 Mint to the Base / Tempo block calls:
  **Cover 4 Palms, 1 Double WR1, Cover 3 Buzz Match, Tampa 2.**

**The 5-second tempo routine** (drop steps if short on clock):

1. Audible, don't browse. LT → one of the four. Never open the full menu vs
   tempo.
2. Glance at your safeties (live bug: some shells misalign — re-audible the
   same play if wrong). Bug also eats macros: they can fire minus one setting,
   so hand-confirm the one adjustment that matters.
3. One adjustment max: shade underneath vs quick game, over top vs shots.
4. Pre-select your user defender — your roaming safety by default. Palms and
   1 Double WR1 exist on this sheet precisely because their shells stay sound
   while you freelance; in Tampa 2 take the hole LB instead.

**Panic buttons, in order:** Cover 4 Palms (never awful vs anything), then
1 Double WR1 (bracket the guy the drive runs through), then Tampa 2
(unbeatable by mesh/drag scripts). Blitzing blind vs tempo is how underdogs
die — only Buck Zone Blitz is acceptable blind pressure (3-deep net).

**Timeouts are tempo-breakers.** The moment tilt rises — he converts and
sprints to the line — burn one. It breaks his rhythm, opens your full
playbook, and resets you. Keep two in the second half for clock; the rest
exist to be spent on composure.

## The cheese list and counters (this cycle)

| Cheese | Counter |
|---|---|
| Gun Bunch audible spam | Cover 3 Match; set match checks vs bunch in adjustments; never press bunch; user the first in-breaker |
| Mesh / crosser spam | Tampa 2 / Tampa 2 Drop; user one drag yourself; never chase a drag with a zone defender |
| Four verts / HB seam | Cover 4 Quarters / Palms; user the HB seam pipe — that's the real read |
| RPO bubble/glance spam | Cover 3 Buzz + RPO Pass Key Conservative; user the conflict defender, show one thing, break the other way |
| Stretch / toss spam | Check to 4-2-5 Over G (SS Blitz 3 / Pinch Blitz) or 3-4 Tite Cover 3 Sky; never sit in mug looks vs outside runs |
| Shift/motion exploits | After any offensive shift, manually check your slot corner (known flip bug); play leverage-and-help |
| Scrambling QB | Cover 1 Contain Spy (Dime 3-2) or Cover 1 Contain Press (4-2-5 Under); Plaster ON; contain ends, never all-out blitz a runner |
| Tempo itself | Post-patch it can't wipe your package. The audible-only routine is the counter. It's a composure weapon; the checklist is the armor. |

## Between plays: the 25-second ritual

1. 0-5s — log, don't feel. One word: "man," "blitz-left," "user-mid."
2. 5-10s — situation names the sheet block. The LIVE highlight already did
   this for you. Read 2-4 options, not forty.
3. 10-15s — pick and commit. A decent call fully executed beats a perfect
   call chosen at 0:03.
4. 15-25s — sheet down, eyes up: shell, box, user, leverage. Milk to ~3. Snap.

Stillness sharpens sight. The sheet exists so the decision is already made and
the 25 seconds go to watching, not choosing.
